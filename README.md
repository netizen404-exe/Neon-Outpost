# NEON OUTPOST: Rift Protocol

**Survive the waves. Upgrade your weapons. Defend the Outpost.**

The galaxy has fallen into chaos, and the Rift is sending increasingly dangerous enemies into your territory. As an elite pilot stationed at NEON OUTPOST, your mission is simple: survive for as long as possible.

Fight through relentless waves of hostile drones, fast-moving attackers, heavily armoured enemies and deadly ranged units. Collect data shards to level up and choose powerful upgrades that shape your playstyle. Boost your firepower, unleash piercing shots, deploy companion drones and repair your armour as the battle intensifies.

Every five waves, face a powerful Rift Core boss that will test your reflexes and strategy. Use your phase dash to escape danger, master your movement and adapt your build to survive the growing threat.

With fast-paced combat, neon sci-fi visuals, escalating difficulty and a score leaderboard, every run is a chance to push further and prove your skills.

**Features**
- **Wave-based survival:** Fight increasingly difficult enemies and see how long you can last.
- **Upgradeable weapons:** Choose upgrades that transform your combat strategy.
- **Epic boss encounters:** Challenge a Rift Core boss every five waves.
- **Fast movement:** Dodge attacks and escape danger with your phase dash.
- **Compete for the top:** Earn points and aim for the highest score on the leaderboard.
- **Play your way:** Keyboard and mouse controls on desktop, with touchscreen support on mobile.

**The Rift is expanding. The Outpost is under attack. How long can you survive?**
